Price Guide

Our Rates

We do our best to keep our pricing simple, transparent, and fair, so you always know what to expect.

When possible we will always try to identify and suggest solutions to help you get things going before we carry out any investigative or repair work. This allows us to gather as much information as possible before any chargeable work begins.

If further diagnosis, testing, or repair is required, our pricing is as follows:

Our standard rate for diagnostics is £25. This gives us time to identify what has gone wrong. In some cases we can fix your issue at this stage. Where a cause of failure has been identified but a repair is not possible, our diagnostic rate will apply to any jobs where a resolution could not be applied due to third party restrictions like your ISP or manuafture specific guidance or if a resolution is not an economical solution.

If it is deemed during the initial diagnostic stage that further work will be required to diagnose or resolve your issue, we will provide you with a total cost before any further work begins.

Call out charge - Where a call out is requred, a call out fee of up to £50 may apply.

In most cases, faults are identified, repaired, or assessed during the initial diagnostics stage, meaning the standard diagnostics price covers the majority of jobs.

Some jobs may require specialist parts, additional labour, travel or a bespoke solution. In these cases, we will provide a tailored quotation based on the work involved. Any specific pricing will be fully discussed and agreed with you before any work is carried out. Where a bespoke quotation has been agreed, our standard rates may not apply.

Please note that the pricing above applies to home and personal customers only. Business support requirements can vary significantly depending on the nature of the work, response times, and ongoing support needs. If you require our services for your business, please get in touch for more information.

Our goal is to provide honest advice, quality workmanship, and fair pricing with no unexpected costs.
