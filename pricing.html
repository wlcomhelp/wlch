Clear and Fair Pricing

Our Rates

We believe in keeping our pricing simple, transparent, and fair, so you always know what to expect.

Every job begins with an initial troubleshooting consultation, which can be carried out over the phone, via message, or in person where required. This allows us to gather as much information as possible before any chargeable work begins.

If further diagnosis, testing, or repair is required, our pricing is as follows:

- Initial diagnosis, investigation, or repair: £25 minimum charge
- Additional time: £20 per 30 minutes thereafter

In most cases, faults are identified, repaired, or assessed during the initial visit, meaning the minimum charge covers the majority of jobs.

Some job may require specialist parts, additional labour, or a bespoke solution. In these cases, we will provide a tailored quotation based on the work involved. Any specific pricing will be fully discussed and agreed with you before any work is carried out. Where a bespoke quotation has been agreed, our standard rates may not apply.

Please note that the pricing above applies to home and personal customers only. Business, commercial, and organisational support requirements can vary significantly depending on the nature of the work, response times, and ongoing support needs. If you require our services for your business, please get in touch for more information.

Our goal is to provide honest advice, quality workmanship, and fair pricing with no unexpected costs.
