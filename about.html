About us

About Me You might notice that throughout this website and on my Facebook page I use the word "we." In the interest of being open and transparent, "we" is actually just me.

I'm an experienced IT professional with a background supporting both business and personal technology, but my passion is helping everyday people get the most from their devices without the confusing technical language.

I believe technology should make life easier, not more stressful. Whether your Wi-Fi keeps dropping out, your laptop has slowed to a crawl, you need help setting up a new device, or you simply have a question about staying safe online, no problem is too small.

My aim is to provide honest, straightforward advice that you can understand and trust. I'll explain things in plain English, recommend solutions that make sense for you, and never expect you to know the latest technical buzzwords.

I'm particularly passionate about supporting my local community and helping people who might not know where to turn when technology becomes frustrating. Sometimes you don't need a repair—you just need someone to answer a question or point you in the right direction.

Whether you're looking for help solving a problem, advice on protecting your online accounts, or simply want to better understand your technology, I'm always happy to help.
